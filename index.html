FAQ

Common questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages. Book online or call to schedule your first exam.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We work with most major providers and will help you understand your cover before treatment.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away. We keep time free every day for urgent cases like severe pain, swelling or a knocked-out tooth.

How often should I visit?

For most people, a check-up and cleaning every six months. We may suggest more often depending on your oral health.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Flexible monthly plans are available for larger treatments such as implants and orthodontics.
