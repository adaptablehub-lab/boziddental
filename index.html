FAQ

Common questions

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages. Book online or call us to schedule your first exam.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We work with most major insurance providers and will help you understand your coverage before treatment. Contact us with your plan details.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call our emergency line right away. We reserve time every day for urgent cases such as severe pain, swelling, or a knocked-out tooth.

How often should I visit the dentist?

For most people, a check-up and cleaning every six months is ideal. We may recommend more frequent visits depending on your oral health.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We offer flexible monthly payment options for larger treatments such as implants and orthodontics.
